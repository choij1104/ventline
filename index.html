Acute deterioration on the ventilator LOCAL · DOPES mnemonic

Checklist order, not a guideline citation. Disconnect from the circuit and bag with 100 % O₂ while working through it.

  • D — Displacement: tube depth at the teeth, ETCO₂ waveform, breath sounds, chest rise
  • O — Obstruction: pass a suction catheter; kinks, secretions, biting
  • P — Pneumothorax: unilateral breath sounds, tracheal shift, hemodynamic collapse → decompress
  • E — Equipment: circuit leak, O₂ supply, ventilator fault, power
  • S — Stacking (auto-PEEP): long expiratory flow at end-expiration → disconnect, lengthen expiratory time, lower rate

VENTLINE v0.1.1 — prototype

Offline, deterministic, citation-linked mechanical ventilation decision support. Same inputs always give the same output. Every engine step carries its source section.

Source

[ARDSnet] NIH NHLBI ARDS Clinical Network. Mechanical Ventilation Protocol Summary (protocol card, rev. 07/2008).

Tagged LOCAL

Swine weight-based defaults, acid–base classification, DOPES checklist, peak-vs-plateau triage. These are editable defaults, not guideline content, and are marked in the UI.

Not in v0.1

Pediatric settings, pressure-support step-down schedule, prone positioning, ECMO referral, neuromuscular blockade dosing, transport ventilator model-specific limits, PFC-specific ventilation guidance. Each will be added only after its primary source is read.

Intended use. Reference and decision-support aid for trained clinicians and researchers. Not a medical device; not reviewed or cleared by FDA. Clinical judgment and the current published protocol govern. No patient data is transmitted; the log stays in this browser and is not a medical record.
