VENTLINE v0.3.0

Offline, deterministic, citation-linked ventilator decision support for paramedics who run mechanical ventilators in transport and prolonged field care. Same inputs give the same output. Every step names its source.

How the engine works

Patient state → rules → guardrails → one next step → timeline. Rules live in modules (CPG 92, ARDSnet, safety). Guardrails clip any proposed change to the tighter of the protocol limit and the ventilator's range, and say so when they do. New modules — hemodynamics, sedation — register into the same engine.

Sources

[CPG92] Joint Trauma System. Mechanical Ventilation Basics. CPG ID 92. 09 Apr 2025 (supersedes 27 Dec 2021).

[ARDSnet] NIH NHLBI ARDS Clinical Network. Mechanical Ventilation Protocol Summary (protocol card, rev. 07/2008).

Where the sources disagree

Body weight: CPG 92 uses 2.2 kg per inch over 60 in (female base 45 kg); ARDSnet uses 2.3 kg per inch (female base 45.5 kg). VENTLINE uses the formula of the selected protocol and shows both. Rate: CPG 92 device range is 10–30/min; ARDSnet allows up to 35. PEEP: CPG 92 range 5–20; the ARDSnet tables reach 24. When a protocol step exceeds the ventilator range, the guardrail stops at the range and flags it.

Tags

LOCAL standard physiology or an editable default, not guideline content. SIM simulator assumption, illustrative only.

Not in v0.3

Pediatric settings, sedation and paralytic dosing (CPG 92 refers to the JTS PFC Analgesia and Sedation CPG), prone positioning, ECMO, pressure-support step-down schedule, hemodynamic module. Each waits for its primary source.

Intended use. Research and decision-support aid for trained clinicians. Not a medical device; not reviewed or cleared by FDA. Clinical judgment, local medical direction, and the current published guideline govern. No patient data leaves this device.
